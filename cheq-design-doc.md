# Cheq: A Social Prediction Game for Tokens

Cheq is a play-money game where people bet on where memecoins are headed. No real money, no crypto wallets, no risk, just points, bragging rights, and the thrill of calling a move before your friends do.

You back a prediction, *"DEGEN doubles by Friday"*, and either challenge a friend head-to-head or throw it open to the crowd. When the deadline hits, the market decides who was right, and the points (and status) follow.

**Positioning:** a *prediction game / fantasy token league*, the fun of degen trading with none of the downside.

---

## Why It Works

Real-money crypto betting is risky, slow to build, and legally fraught. Stripping the money out removes every one of those problems and keeps the part people actually enjoy: the call, the rivalry, the win.

The catch with play money is that stakes feel low, so the game has to make *status* the real currency. Cheq does that through friend rivalries, streaks, badges, and seasonal leaderboards. You're not playing to get rich; you're playing to be right, and to prove it.

---

## The Experience

Cheq has two ways to play. One is the heart of the product; the other brings the crowd.

### Private, the heart
Challenge a friend directly. You each put points on the line, the winner takes the pot, and every matchup adds to your running head-to-head record against them. This is where the game lives, a group chat's worth of ongoing rivalries, callouts, and rematches. It's the reason people come back.

### Public, the crowd
Open a prediction to everyone. Anyone can take a side, everyone who's right shares the winnings, and the biggest calls climb the global feed. This is the shop window, how new players discover the game and get pulled in.

---

## What You Can Bet On

Predictions start simple and grow over time. The opening lineup:

- **Will it hit the number?** "DEGEN reaches $0.05 by Friday." The classic call.
- **Up or down?** "DEGEN over the next 24 hours." One tap, instant action, the everyday bet.
- **Head-to-head** "DEGEN vs. WIF, which pumps harder?" Two tokens enter, one wins. The natural way to challenge a friend.

Room to grow later: over/unders on volume or market cap, price-range brackets, "which token hits the milestone first" races, and multi-bet parlays for the bold.

---

## Progression & Status

Two things move as you play:

- **Points**, your betting balance. They rise and fall with your calls, and everyone gets topped up so a bad run never locks you out.
- **Level**, your standing. It only ever goes *up*, earned just by playing, so even a losing streak still feels like progress.

On top of that sits the status layer that keeps people hooked:

- **Badges** for the moments worth remembering: first win, hot streaks, an underdog upset, a comeback from nothing.
- **Seasons** that reset the leaderboard on a cycle, so there's always a fresh race and newcomers can climb.
- **Rivalries**, your win-loss record against each friend, turning every duel into the next chapter of a running story.
- **Leaderboards & a live feed** showing who's winning big right now.

---

## Where to Start

Build the private game first: the friend duels, the points, the rivalries, the core progression. That's the loop that proves the product and demos beautifully: challenge a friend, watch it resolve live, settle the score.

The public crowd game comes next as the growth layer, once the core loop is fun on its own.
